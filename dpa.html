Data Processing Agreement

Effective: August 2026 — Pilot Phase

Overview

This Data Processing Agreement ("DPA") describes how agntai.app collects, processes, stores, and protects your real estate data when you use our service.

  • Data Controller (You): The real estate agent or broker using agntai.app
  • Data Processor (Us): agntai.app and its operators
  • Relationship: You own your data. We process it on your behalf to provide the service.

What Data We Collect

When you use agntai.app, we collect and store:

  • Contact Information: Lead names, phone numbers, email addresses
  • Transaction Data: Property addresses, prices, dates, status
  • Communications: Emails, notes, call summaries
  • Account Information: Your login, preferences, usage history
  • AI Interactions: Questions you ask the AI assistant and its responses

How We Use Your Data

We use your data only to:

  • Provide the agntai.app service to you
  • Improve the service through usage analytics (anonymized, no personal details shared)
  • Fix bugs and provide customer support
  • Comply with legal requirements

We never sell your data to third parties, share your leads with other users, use your data to train AI models, or delete your data without permission.

How We Protect Your Data

Security Measures

  • Encryption: Data transmitted via HTTPS (SSL/TLS); sensitive data encrypted at rest
  • Database Security: Supabase PostgreSQL with Row-Level Security (RLS) — only you can access your own data
  • Access Control: Only our team can access systems for support; no access to user data without permission
  • Regular Backups: Automatic daily backups to prevent data loss

What This Means: Your leads and contacts are locked down so that other users cannot see your data, our team cannot accidentally see your data, and data is protected against unauthorized access.

Data Retention & Deletion

During Your Account

  • Your data is stored as long as your account is active
  • You can request data deletion or export at any time

After You Cancel

  • You have 30 days to export your data
  • After 30 days, all data is permanently deleted from our systems
  • Backup copies are deleted within 90 days

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access all your data (we can export it in CSV format)
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Delete your data (within 30 days of account cancellation)
  • Understand how we process it (this DPA)
  • Object to processing (we'll stop processing and delete it)

To exercise these rights: Email kzurfazekas@gmail.com with your request.

International Compliance

GDPR (European Users)

  • If you or your contacts are in the EU, this DPA meets GDPR requirements
  • We do not transfer EU data outside the EU
  • You are responsible for legal compliance with your own leads

CCPA & State Privacy Laws

  • We comply with California and other state privacy requirements
  • We do not sell personal information
  • We respond to data deletion requests within 45 days

Third-Party Services

We use the following services to operate agntai.app:

Service Purpose Data Shared
Supabase Database & authentication Your account info, leads, contacts
Claude (Anthropic) AI suggestions Only the text you ask about; not stored by Anthropic
Netlify Web hosting No personal data

Claude Data Handling

When you ask the AI a question, the text is sent to Claude's API, processed, and deleted. Claude does not train models on your data or store it.

Data Breach Notification

If we discover unauthorized access to your data, we will:

  • Notify you within 72 hours
  • Describe what data was affected
  • Explain what we're doing to fix it
  • Provide guidance on next steps

Changes to This DPA

We may update this DPA as our service grows. If we make material changes (like sharing data with new third parties), we'll notify you via email and in the app.
